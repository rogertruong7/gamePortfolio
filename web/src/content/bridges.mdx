## What it is

Bridges was a UNSW DevSoc project to connect student societies with potential sponsors. Sponsors would be able to browse societies and events without logging in, and societies would manage their profiles and events from a dashboard. I was a subcommittee member on the 2025 founding team.

The project didn't reach its MVP and the repository is now archived, so there's no public release to try.

## How it was built

- **Client:** Next.js with the App Router, with Google sign-in through NextAuth.
- **Server:** Express 5 in TypeScript, with public routes for listing societies, reading a society and listing its events.
- **Database:** PostgreSQL with Kysely. Types are generated from the database, and the initial migration models societies, social links, categories, sponsors, sponsorships and events.
- **Local setup:** Docker Compose for the services.

When a route returns a society, it strips private fields such as the login email and approval flags before responding, and it joins in social links and sponsors.
