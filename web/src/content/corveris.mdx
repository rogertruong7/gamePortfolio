## About Corveris

Corveris, based in San Francisco, builds an AI crime reporting platform. Residents call or message it directly, and it asks follow-up questions, collects evidence and prepares a report for the police department to review.

I worked there for eight months as a forward-deployed engineer through Lyra, and as the primary full-stack engineer during my first few months. I worked across the FastAPI backend, the React officer dashboard, the Next.js citizen app and the Terraform infrastructure, as the platform was deployed to 8+ law enforcement departments.

## One-click checks on AI summaries

An officer shouldn't have to take an AI summary on trust. I made each summary verifiable in one click by linking its statements back to the conversation they came from:

- **Typed citation schemas** give summaries a structured way to point at their sources.
- **Transcript turn IDs** map a citation to the exact turn in the conversation.
- **Unicode-aware text-span matching** finds the precise words to highlight, including in transcripts that aren't in English.
- **Memoised reverse indexes** make navigation work in both directions: from summary to transcript, and from transcript back to summary.

This works across both the English and original-language transcripts in the dashboard.

## Penal codes by jurisdiction

The same incident maps to different law depending on where it happened. A vehicle break-in needs both a national NIBRS classification and the right section of the state's penal code. I built jurisdiction-specific penal code resolution for California and Texas:

1. YAML configuration holds each jurisdiction's rules.
2. NIBRS-compliant overrides correct the classification where needed.
3. LLM-powered sub-classifiers handle the harder distinctions within theft, fraud, vandalism, assault and hit-and-run incidents.

This improved report accuracy by 25% and cut officer review time by 30 minutes per incident.

## Dashboard performance

Tables with 400+ active reports were slow to load. I improved load performance by 45% with changes on both sides:

- **Queries.** Paginated report queries no longer depend on full-table metadata scans.
- **Database.** I added PostgreSQL indexes and server-side aggregation caching.
- **Client data.** TanStack Query caches results and keeps page data while the next page loads.
- **Rendering.** TanStack Virtual only renders the table rows on screen.

## Claiming a report by SMS

Anonymous reporting keeps the process easy for citizens, but it also opens the door to fraud. I built a claim-via-SMS workflow that keeps reporting easy while tying a report to a verified phone:

- Twilio OTP verification
- Rate-limited, single-use claim tokens that expire after 72 hours
- Constant-time comparison for URL-safe Base64 identity binding

This reduced anonymous report fraud by 70%.
