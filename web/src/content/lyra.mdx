## About Lyra

Lyra Technologies is a digital product studio based in Sydney and Melbourne. Since 2023 it has partnered with Silicon Valley startups to design, build and ship products. Its site lists 75+ client engagements and 15+ Y Combinator companies.

## What I do

I'm employed by Lyra and placed with one client at a time as a forward-deployed engineer. In practice that means working as a member of the startup's engineering team on whatever the product needs: backend services, frontends, infrastructure or AI pipelines.

## What carries across

The stacks differ from client to client, from Django and Vue at Cognimark to FastAPI and React at AndCo and Corveris. A few things have come up at all three:

- **Domains with real rules.** Healthcare records, police reporting and legal work each have their own standards and paperwork, and the software has to fit them.
- **Features that run end to end.** My work has usually crossed the API, the UI and the deployment rather than sitting in one layer.
- **Software that handles sensitive data.** Patient records, crime reports and legal cases all need care in who can see what.
